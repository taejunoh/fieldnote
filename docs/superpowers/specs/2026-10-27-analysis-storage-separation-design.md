## Boundaries

- Assess the matchup and observed price using available current evidence. A missing explicit pregame status, fixture identifier, exact start minute, or structured-record source admission does not by itself prohibit a clearly qualified body assessment.
- Keep an initial assessment, an actionable GO/BET decision, and a persistable structured record distinct. A price PASS requires an actual price comparison; missing matchup evidence remains incomplete. WAIT identifies the material unresolved condition and reassessment trigger.
- Do not equate a future official schedule lacking a machine-readable pregame field with a started game. Preserve the known schedule and the specific status uncertainty. Check fresh state for contradictions; an inaccessible or empty live feed proves neither started nor unstarted.
- Preserve existing GO/BET timing, policy, source, citation, identity and provenance validation. Never create actual bets from recommendations. Never relabel known started/finished games as pregame or backdate a new judgment.
- Exclude retrospective hypothetical assessments from current structured event/recommendation arrays. Where only a record admission condition fails, preserve the substantive answer and state the exact storage limitation rather than replacing every judgment with EVALUATION INCOMPLETE or clearing unrelated eligible records.
- A missing final lineup is a matchup-specific variable, not a universal ban on initial assessment or EARLY GO. Apply the adopted common and sport criteria; add no blanket approval gate or new unit rules.
